# Verified draft correlation and partial SMTP outcomes

Local-only mail is adopted only by a selected server UID identity or one unique matching RFC Message-ID in the same account/folder. Subject and a nearby timestamp cannot establish identity. Conflicting, missing or ambiguous IDs keep separate copies, preserving local text and recipients. Servers that omit usable identities may therefore show duplicates instead of risking a destructive merge.

SMTP acceptance is per recipient. Normalize and retain accepted/rejected addresses and rejection status codes as a receipt, then persist that receipt before Sent filing. A scheduled partial result enters `partial`, remains visible with its content and receipt, and is never automatically eligible for another submission. A direct partial send creates the same nonqueued recovery record and returns a visible partial result. Its compose window remains an inspectable receipt with send controls locked, rather than closing with a generic Sent status. A receipt-storage failure still returns the known partial outcome, keeps an existing draft, and asks the user to retain/copy the content; it never repeats SMTP to repair local storage.

Recovery requires explicit review. It creates an unsent draft containing only verified rejected recipients in their original To/Cc/Bcc roles, excluding every accepted address. Unmatched or unavailable recipient details produce an incomplete/unaddressed draft and a manual-review warning rather than guessing. Body and local attachments are retained. Dismiss/Undo preserves the partial state and receipt. Bot request-id receipts also persist and replay per-recipient details, including a partial warning, without sending again.

`scripts/check-draft-partial-delivery.js` uses temporary SQLite, fake Nodemailer/IMAP, renderer VM fixtures and dedicated crash/restart processes. It covers conflicting/absent/ambiguous RFC IDs, confirmed adoption, repeated sync and reset, direct/scheduled partial outcomes, Bcc-safe recovery, repeated actions, receipt failures and restart without replay. Bot bridge and native Electron fixtures cover idempotent partial responses and the visible, locked compose receipt. No real account data or mail is used.
