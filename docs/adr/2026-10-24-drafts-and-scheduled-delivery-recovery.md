# Local drafts and durable scheduled delivery

UIDVALIDITY invalidates server UIDs, not local-only mail. Generation resets remove only rows with UID > 0 and their indexes. UID <= 0 drafts retain their text, recipients, attachments, blob references and search entries, just as ordinary UID reconciliation already preserves them. They can be uploaded later from the same local draft.

Scheduled delivery persists a claim and Message-ID before any external submission. Its states are pending (not attempted), sending (claimed), sent (SMTP accepted), failed (definitely not sent), and unknown (delivery could not be confirmed). Only pending is eligible for the automatic pump. A proven connection/envelope/authentication refusal or local preflight validation can fail as not sent; an ambiguous transport result cannot. Sent acceptance is committed immediately after SMTP returns, before best-effort Sent filing awaits IMAP. Filing/cleanup failure must not downgrade that acceptance or put the message back in the automatic queue.

Startup changes interrupted sending rows to unknown. Interrupted sent rows retain the payload and acceptance record for review. Both remain visible in Scheduled mail and never resend automatically. A legacy pending/failed row has no durable evidence of its prior submission state; adding the Message-ID column moves those existing rows to unknown once, preserving contents and asking the user to check Sent after the safety upgrade. Modern pending rows retain normal scheduling across restart.

The UI distinguishes not sent, uncertain delivery and SMTP accepted, and labels review actions Recover draft and Dismiss record. Recovery requires an explicit duplicate-risk confirmation, then atomically saves the payload and attachments as an unsent local draft; it never schedules or sends it. The handoff retains a Check Sent warning. Dismissing removes only the recovery record, not any delivered mail. Undo preserves unknown/sent status and Message-ID instead of requeuing it. Direct recovery without acknowledgement and rescheduling/downgrading uncertain records are refused. Known-unsent failures can be edited or explicitly retried.

`scripts/check-draft-scheduled-recovery.js` uses real temporary SQLite, synthetic transports and dedicated child processes terminated before SMTP or during Sent filing after acceptance. It verifies restart without replay, saved data/search/attachments across generation changes, claim/receipt storage failure, ambiguous versus refused delivery, repeated recovery and dismiss/Undo, migration and UI warnings. Native renderer fixtures cover the new delivery labels/actions. SMTP exactly-once delivery is not assumed: a stable Message-ID alone cannot make an uncertain delivery safe to repeat.
