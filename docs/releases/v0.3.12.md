## Bounded mail work

Mail sync and related work stay bounded: credential reads stay async,
recipient aggregates use header deltas, sync batches yield, and
invalidations stay scoped. Outbox summaries, coalesced reads, and body
backfill keep Calendar, Agent, and UI retention.

## Folders, labels, and Support rail

Folders and labels keep provider organize identity. Support-rail Open in
Mail navigation is hardened.

## Unread chip, bulk Undo, Agent, and Out of Office

The All Account unread chip uses the primary-mailbox unread sum and stays
fresh while you view Secondary or other folders. Bulk Archive and Trash
run in non-blocking chunks with accumulated Undo and Details across
batches. Agent navigation is cache-first: the overlay shows only on a
cold load or Refresh. All Account Out of Office opens a mailbox chooser
instead of silently picking one account.

## Not in this version

The Electron/Node security bump is not in this version.
