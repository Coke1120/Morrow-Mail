## All Account Mail

All Account Mail is a collapsible section. Choosing the header expands or
collapses it and does not open a list. Inbox opens the same merged list and
keeps the Unread chip. Gmail still uses cached All Mail; other accounts use
Inbox. Outbox stays beside Inbox as the local undo queue.

## Reading header

The open message shows its date and time on the From row, right-aligned, in
the main reading pane and in a separate reader window. That text uses the
reader's existing date and time format. Loading and download-error headers
use the same row. To and Cc stay on the lines below.

## Agent cards

Agent Todos includes List and Card. List stays the default. Card shows the
sender, subject, one Agent reason line, and Reply and Done. Hover, Tab
focus, or a tap opens the same detail: a fuller summary, the classification
reason, one thread line, and Reply, Done, and Dismiss. Escape or leaving the
card dismisses it. The page header does not repeat that detail. Opening the
detail does not open the message or complete the todo.

## Learned triage

Settings → Learned, under Agent, lists explicit moves among Needs You, FYI,
and Other, repeated Mark as read or a successful reply for one sender or
thread, and opened or dismissed suggestion labels. The same pattern is
Learned after 3 times. The list is kept in `agent-learning.json` on this
computer. Delete removes that pattern and its count, and an action that
already started does not recreate it. Learned rows do not rewrite Brain
categories, triage prompts, or mailbox rules, and they do not send mail,
mark mail read, or complete a todo. A Learned category stays a hint until
that message is moved.
