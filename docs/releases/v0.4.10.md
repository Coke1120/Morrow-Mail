# Downloaded-view filtering, editable replies and Shift selection

This release promotes merged PRs #559, #560 and #562.

## Changes

- **Filter downloaded view** matches a literal sender, subject or preview phrase
  across all downloaded mail in the selected folder or view before count, sort
  and pagination. Connected accounts and Local Folders retain 200-message pages,
  current scope and filter-hidden selections. Typing, clearing and navigation
  reject obsolete responses; failures offer retry. It does not search mail that
  has not been downloaded or expand the sync-history setting. Toolbar search
  remains limited to its returned results, and Outbox to loaded rows.
- Fresh **Reply** and **Reply All** put the original and available earlier cached
  conversation text in the editable Message field. Edit or delete that text,
  including the entire quote. Save, Preview Send, Send and Schedule use the
  edited message without appending the original again. The separate history pane
  is reference only. Late history reads, AI writing and signature changes preserve
  quote edits. Saved/recovered drafts, Forward and Bot proposal behavior remain
  unchanged. Earlier cached history is not automatically added to authored AI
  input; the existing selected-source reply context is unchanged.
- **Shift-click** selects both endpoints and all visible intervening rows from
  the last clicked anchor, in either direction. Checkbox clicks share the same
  behavior; repeated Shift-clicks extend or shrink the range. Ctrl/Cmd remains
  additive. Ranges follow the displayed order on the loaded page only, and do not
  include hidden or unloaded mail. Paging, search, Clear/Escape and navigation
  retire the anchor. Shift selection does not open or mark the target read.

## Validation and limits

The three feature PRs passed Linux and Windows CI on their final heads. Synthetic
host and renderer regressions cover multi-page filtering, Local Folders, response
races, edited/deleted quotes, AI/signature interactions, frozen output payloads,
and inclusive selection across layouts. Release candidate gates are the full
aggregate check, the integrated promotion CI and the paired release workflow.

Tests use synthetic mail and IPC. Real mailbox providers, live AI, native app
interaction and installed-app updater transitions are not certified by these
checks. Filtering covers downloaded data only, and Shift ranges remain within a
loaded page.

Windows remains unsigned. macOS remains ad hoc signed, without Developer ID or
notarization. The paired workflow verifies provenance, asset hashes, updater
feeds, native Mac archive integrity and the Ed25519 manifest before publication.
Those checks do not establish Gatekeeper trust or authorize installation.
Existing releases and signing roots remain unchanged.
