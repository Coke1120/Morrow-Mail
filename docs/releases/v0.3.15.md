## All Account Inbox Out of Office for the selected mailbox

All Account Inbox shows the selected mailbox Out of Office status, including
compact `Out of Office: off` and Manage.

## Large-mailbox startup phases and checkpoint policy

Startup preparation runs in named phases. Body-metadata fill stays within a
per-transaction budget. Checkpoint policy scales with mailbox size.

## Runtime search projection, scoped summaries, and keyset paging

Local search projects metadata for large mailboxes. Summary staging stays
scoped. Unfiltered list pages use validated keyset pagination.

## Not in this version

The Electron/Node security bump is not in this version.
