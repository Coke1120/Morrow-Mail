## Timeline sheet in the reader action bar

Timeline is one button beside Summary in the opened-message action bar. Choosing
it opens a sheet with optional free-text keywords and editable contact emails
prefilled from that message's From, To, Cc and Reply-To. Those fields guide
summaries; every included downloaded thread message still goes to AI. Title,
supported language and timezone default from the subject and system. Only
Generate starts AI after explicit-action AI data permission. Opening the sheet,
saved history and reviewed exports use no AI.

Message/Timeline tabs and any duplicate Generate timeline entry are removed.
Right, Bottom and Separate window layouts keep the reader intact. The freeze
window remains the opened message and its downloaded thread—not list selection
or search. Missing, incomplete, warned or unsupported messages still need
conditional partial-coverage acceptance. Coverage limits, consent, provenance,
uncertainty review and local persistence are unchanged from v0.3.23.

Reviewed Markdown, isolated PDF and public JSON schema 1.0.0 exports still
require a fresh personal-information review. URL/webhook export, bot/CLI
operations and scheduled generation remain unavailable.

## Subject reply and forward icons

Cached reply and forward flags appear as compact icons before the Subject in
the mail list instead of a separate Reply/forward column. Column visibility,
sort order and keyboard reordering keep their previous behavior for remaining
columns.

## Integration and validation limits

Chromium synthetic checks cover reader action parity (including Timeline),
subject-prefix icons, sheet open/generate/cancel paths, light/dark themes and
all three layouts. Schema, source selection, extraction, consent, persistence
and exports remain synthetic. Publication uses the existing Windows-then-macOS
pipeline. Windows remains unsigned; macOS remains ad hoc signed without
Developer ID or notarization. Live mail accounts, real AI output and installed
updater transitions remain unverified. No signing roots, secrets or release
gates are changed.
