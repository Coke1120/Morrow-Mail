## Learned follow-ups

Learned waits for matching completed read journal settlements before counting
a pattern or completing an opt-in read-and-done todo. Validated thread identity
is preserved across fresh triage. Reply learning no longer depends on a Needs
You todo target. Already-open suggestion surfaces refresh when learning changes
or a pattern is deleted. Pattern IDs stay stable across list and delete.

## Undo details and Bodies Sync

Undo Details keeps pending rows, checkbox state, and focus while restores
finish. Late restores do not affect a replacement Undo offer. Scheduled
cancellation restores consume only their captured Undo offer. Outbox behavior
is unchanged.

The Bodies Sync tooltip keeps a hover bridge outside its scrollport, contained
wheel scrolling, and a keyboard-focusable scroll area. Confirmed IMAP
source-limit results show as Limited with No action needed. The existing
3 MiB limit and actionable download errors stay.

## Mail, Agent, and Settings polish

Uncertain Outbox deliveries stay durable and noneditable. Agent filing follows
matching journal completion without overwriting newer store state. Bot Disable
and Revoke keep their intent during probes. Unsupported CSS image functions are
restricted. Features, help, and ADR notes stay in sync with these behaviors.

## Compose, Brain, and Calendar polish

Compose rejects finished AI events, fences pending attachments and stale
recipient choices, and saves an inert recovery draft before Undo opens
Compose. Brain keeps concurrent saved rules and Unicode facts, and rejects
obsolete Fix or analysis results. Calendar keeps original instants on non-time
edits, exposes reauthorization after known auth failure, and sorts actual
instants across offsets.
